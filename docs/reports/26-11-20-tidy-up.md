## Implemented boundary

Upstream rewrote Tidy up in four commits (`cfd6bf52`, `d3f7ae90`, `a7a9e627`, `1a68ded5`) to follow what was measured in the target application. [tidy.ts](../../src/engine/tidy.ts) is a direct port of the native module as it stands at the pin:

- Tidy up keeps the order and the sizes of every layer and keeps the first layer where it is.
- A row gets one horizontal gap and keeps every Y; a column is the mirror image. Layers may overlap, also in a chain.
- The gap is the one two neighbours already share most often, else the mean rounded half up. A negative gap is raised so that no layer hides the one two before it.
- A grid with one layer in every cell is packed along its longer axis, each layer centred in its column or row.
- Fewer than three layers, a diagonal, an irregular layout, a grid with an empty place and layers in an auto layout flow are left alone, with no undo step.

[`arrange.tidy`](../../src/engine/arrange.ts) uses it, refuses a spacing beyond one million, and passes the view's Snap to pixel grid. The [Arrange panel](../../src/ArrangePanel.tsx) offers Tidy up only where it applies.

Before, the port placed any two or more layers on a grid of the largest width and height from the selection's top-left, with a gap of at least 10.
