Scope notes

The provider's subscribed callback substitutes for the native host's READY signal, and presence travels as full samples instead of individual signals. A resumed sample can carry a retained spotlight before the host reads signals again. The pointer and follow commands are not gated, as in native. This self-contained report uses no remote assets or scripts; evidence links require the checkout.
