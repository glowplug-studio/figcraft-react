## Native contract

`crates/engine/src/selection/tidy.rs` at the pin, a rewrite that the four commits built up:

- Tidy up keeps the order and the sizes of every layer and keeps the first layer where it is.
- Rows are the groups of layers whose Y ranges overlap, transitively; columns the groups whose X ranges overlap. Layers in one group must also overlap each other pairwise on the other axis.
- **A row** is one row group over two or more column groups. Its horizontal gaps are evened and every Y kept. A column is the mirror image. Layers of a row may overlap along X, also in a chain; a negative gap counts.
- **The one gap** is the explicit spacing; else a single gap; else the most common gap, the smaller of two equally common ones; else the mean rounded to a whole number, half up.
- **A negative gap is raised** so that every layer starts after the end of the layer two before it (`floor(-w / 2) + 1` for the narrowest interior width) and the second starts after the first (`floor(-w0) + 1`). An explicit spacing is not raised.
- **A grid** is two or more rows and two or more columns with one layer in every cell. It is packed along the axis with more groups, columns on a tie: each unit is as wide as its widest layer around where its layers were, the units are evened, every layer is centred in its unit, and each unit is stacked from the selection's start. With Snap to pixel grid on, positions along the packing axis are rounded half up.
- **Unavailable**: fewer than three layers (two with an explicit spacing), more than 2,000, a layer in an auto layout flow, layers all in one row and one column, a diagonal, a grid with an empty place, any other layout. Nothing moves and no undo step is added. A selected parent stands for its selected descendants.
- The command refuses a spacing beyond one million either way.

`crates/ui-egui/src/panels/right.rs` offers Tidy up only where `can_tidy` holds.
